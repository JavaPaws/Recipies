# Creamy Garlic Butter Beef Roast with Golden Baby Potatoes

## Ingredients
### For the beef
- 1.5–2 kg beef roast (chuck, sirloin, or rib roast)
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1½ tsp salt
- 1 tsp black pepper
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp dried thyme
- 1 tsp dried rosemary
- 4–5 cloves garlic, minced

### For the creamy garlic butter sauce
- 3 tbsp butter
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- ½ cup heavy cream
- ½ cup beef broth
- ½ cup grated Parmesan
- 1 tsp Italian seasoning
- 1 tbsp chopped fresh parsley
- Salt & black pepper, to taste

### Potatoes
- 700 g baby potatoes
- 1½ tbsp olive oil
- 1 tbsp melted butter
- ½ tsp garlic powder
- ½ tsp paprika
- Salt & pepper

## Method
1. **Prepare the beef:** Pat the roast dry. Rub with the olive oil, salt, pepper, paprika, thyme, rosemary and minced garlic.
2. **Sear:** Heat a large oven-safe pan over medium-high heat. Sear the beef 3–4 minutes per side until nicely browned.
3. **Prepare the potatoes:** Toss the baby potatoes with the olive oil, melted butter, garlic powder, paprika, salt and pepper. Arrange them around the beef.
4. **Roast:** Place in an oven preheated to 180°C (350°F). Roast until the beef reaches your preferred internal temperature; for a juicy medium roast, aim for about 60–65°C (140–150°F) before resting.
5. **Make the sauce:** Melt the butter in a saucepan. Add the garlic and cook about 30 seconds. Add the beef broth and cream and simmer gently for 2–3 minutes.
6. Stir in the Parmesan and Italian seasoning. Cook until the sauce is thick, smooth and creamy. Season with salt and pepper.
7. **Finish:** Remove the beef and let it rest 15–20 minutes. Spoon the sauce generously over the top.
8. Garnish with the chopped parsley and serve with the potatoes.

## Notes
- For a well-browned crust, sear the beef very well before roasting, and don't skip the rest.
- Slice the roast against the grain for maximum tenderness.
- The parsley is listed under the sauce ingredients but used as the garnish in step 8.
