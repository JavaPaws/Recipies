## Ingredients
- 1 lb chicken cutlets
- 1 cup Italian seasoned panko bread crumbs
- ½ cup Parmesan cheese
- 1 tsp salt & pepper
- 1 egg, beaten
- 8 pieces garlic bread (hashtag: #TexasToast)
- ¾ cup pasta sauce
- 8 slices mozzarella "pear" (as written; probably mozzarella pearl)
- For topping: Italian seasoning, balsamic sandwich dressing, basil, etc.
- Oil for frying (if frying; amount not given)

## Method
1. Mix the panko, Parmesan, salt and pepper.
2. Dip the chicken into the egg, let it drip, then cover it in the panko mix.
3. Fry in oil at 350°F, or air fry, or bake.
4. Bake the garlic toast at 400°F for 6 minutes, or until crispy.
5. Slice the chicken into bite-size strips and stuff them into the toast. Top each with a spoonful of sauce and a slice of mozzarella.
6. Sprinkle with Italian seasoning and bake at 350°F for 5 minutes, or until the cheese has melted.
