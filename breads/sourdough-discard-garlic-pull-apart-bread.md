# Sourdough Discard Garlic Pull Apart Bread

- **Servings:** 8
- **Times:** prep 25 min · cook 40 min · rise 2 h 15 min · total 3 h 20 min

## Equipment
- Kitchen scale
- 9x5-inch loaf pan
- Spatula
- Kitchen thermometer (optional)
- Stand mixer with dough hook (optional; see notes)

## Ingredients
### For the bread
- ¼ cup non-dairy or regular dairy milk, warmed to 110℉
- 1 ½ teaspoons instant yeast
- 2 teaspoons granulated sugar
- 2 cups + 1 Tablespoon all-purpose flour
- 200 grams (about ¾ cup) sourdough discard, unfed, at room temperature
- 1 egg, lightly beaten
- 1 Tablespoon fresh parsley or rosemary, chopped
- 1 teaspoon kosher salt
- 1 teaspoon garlic powder
- 2 Tablespoons unsalted butter, melted

### For the topping
- 7 Tablespoons unsalted butter, softened
- 1 Tablespoon fresh parsley or rosemary, chopped
- 1 Tablespoon garlic powder
- ½ teaspoon kosher salt
- Flaky sea salt, for topping

## Method
1. Combine the warmed milk, instant yeast, and sugar in the bowl of a stand mixer fitted with a dough hook. Add the flour, sourdough discard, beaten egg, chopped herbs, salt, and garlic powder. With the mixer on low speed, slowly add the 2 Tablespoons of melted butter. Increase the speed to medium and mix until a shaggy dough forms.
2. Transfer the dough to a smooth work surface and knead until smooth, about 3–4 minutes. The dough should be smooth and slightly tacky (but not sticky). If needed, add more flour or milk (1 Tablespoon / 10 grams at a time) to reach your desired consistency.
3. Transfer the dough to a large greased bowl and cover with plastic wrap. Let rise for 60–90 minutes, or until doubled in size.
4. While the dough rises, make the topping. In a small bowl, combine the softened butter, chopped herbs, garlic powder, and ½ teaspoon salt. Mix until combined. Set aside 1 Tablespoon of this mixture to brush on the loaf when it comes out of the oven. Grease a 9x5-inch loaf pan.
5. After the rise, turn the dough onto a smooth work surface and divide it into 12 equal-sized pieces (about 55 grams each, using a kitchen scale).
6. Flatten each piece with the palm of your hand into a rough circle about 4 inches in diameter, then spread with 1–2 teaspoons of the butter mixture. Fold the piece in half like a taco, then place it in the pan fold-side down. Repeat with the remaining pieces, using all of the butter mixture except the reserved 1 Tablespoon.
7. Cover the pan with plastic wrap or a clean kitchen towel and let rise 30–45 minutes until the dough is puffed. The dough should not rise above the edges of the pan.
8. Preheat the oven to 350℉ (175℃).
9. Bake for 30 minutes, then cover with aluminum foil and bake an additional 5–10 minutes. The loaf will be golden brown, and the internal temperature will reach about 200℉ (95℃).
10. While the loaf is still warm, brush with the reserved butter mixture and sprinkle with flaky sea salt. Let it cool in the pan for 10 minutes, then serve.

## Notes
- **No stand mixer:** mix the dough in a large bowl with a wooden spoon, then knead by hand.
- **Doubling or tripling:** use 2 or 3 9x5-inch pans.
- **Active starter instead of discard:** omit the yeast and use 200g of active starter in place of the discard. The rise times will be longer; the creator gives no exact times, so watch the dough.
- The starter should be fed at a 1:1 water:flour ratio.
- **Variations from the creator:**
  - Sprinkle ¼ cup shredded parmesan between the layers.
  - Fresh garlic works: 1 clove = ¼ tsp garlic powder.
  - Dried herbs work: 1 tsp dried = 1 Tbsp fresh.
  - Sage works as the herb.
- The creator says an overnight cold proof before the second rise "should work" (her answer in the comments).
- **Storage:**
  - Airtight at room temperature for up to 3 days; microwave about 30 seconds to rewarm.
  - Freeze after baking (not before) for up to 3 months. Thaw overnight in the fridge, then microwave 30–60 seconds, or warm the whole loaf at 350℉ for about 10 minutes.
- Best served warm.
