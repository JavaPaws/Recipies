# Sourdough Monkey Bread

- **Servings:** 12
- **Times:** prep 30 min · cook 30 min · fermentation 1 day 14 h · total 1 day 15 h (includes the overnight levain and cold bulk ferment)

## Equipment
- Mixer
- Bench scraper
- 10-inch bundt pan

## Ingredients
### Levain (1:10:10, overnight or about 12 hours)
- 10g ripe sourdough starter (about 2 tsp)
- 100g all-purpose flour (scant ¾ cup)
- 100g water (about 7 Tbsp)

### Dough
- 200g levain (about ¾ cup)
- 240g whole milk (about 1 cup)
- 30g unsalted butter, melted and cooled (about 2 Tbsp)
- 55g granulated sugar (¼ cup)
- 500g bread flour (about 3⅓ cups)
- 15g salt (about 2 tsp)

### Topping
- 230g brown sugar (about 1 cup packed)
- 7g ground cinnamon (about 1 Tbsp)
- 113g unsalted butter, melted (½ cup)
- Pinch of salt

### Icing (optional)
- 65g powdered sugar (½ cup)
- 15g whole milk (1.5 Tbsp)

## Method
1. **Levain (night before):** Mix the ripe starter with the flour and water. Cover and leave in a warm (78°F) place for about 10–12 hours, until bubbly, ripe, doubled and slightly sour-smelling.
2. **Knead:** In a stand mixer, combine the ripe levain, whole milk, melted butter, sugar, bread flour and salt. Knead about 8 minutes until smooth and tacky, or about 10 minutes by hand until smooth and cohesive. If it's too sticky, add flour 10g at a time.
3. **Bulk ferment:** Cover and leave in a warm place (78–80°F) for about 4 hours, until puffed and slightly risen. For more sour flavor, go another 1–2 hours.
4. **Cold bulk ferment:** Refrigerate overnight; the dough can stay in the fridge up to 48 hours. To bake the same day, skip this step and go straight to shaping.
5. **Prepare the pan:** Brush melted butter into every crevice of a 10-inch bundt pan, using the melted butter from the topping.
6. **Topping:** Mix the brown sugar, cinnamon and a pinch of salt. Set aside.
7. **Shape:** Turn out the cold dough. Cut it into 8 sections, then cut each section into 6–8 pieces, for about 50–60 pieces total. Roll each piece into a ball, dunk it in the butter and then in the topping mixture, coating it completely. Layer the balls in the pan until all the dough is used, then pour any leftover butter and topping over the top.
8. **Proof:** Cover and leave in a warm place (she uses 82°F) for about 3–4 hours. An oven with the light on works if you don't have a proofer. It's ready when a ball feels airy and light (no longer cold) and springs back slightly when pressed. It won't necessarily fill the pan. If it's not ready, give it another 30–60 minutes.
9. **Bake:** Preheat the oven to 350°F and bake 30–35 minutes, uncovered. Rest 5–10 minutes in the pan, then turn out onto a large plate. If it doesn't hold its shape, leave it in the pan a little longer.
10. **Icing (optional):** Whisk the powdered sugar and milk and drizzle over the warm bread. Serve warm.

## Notes
- **Christmas-morning make-ahead (from the recipe notes):**
  - Shape the night before and let it rise at 78°F for about 10–12 hours, then bake in the morning.
  - Or let the shaped bread rise most of the way, cover, refrigerate overnight, and bake straight from the fridge.
  - Cooler kitchen (around 74–75°F): mix in the evening, skip the bulk ferment, shape right away, and let it rise on the counter overnight. Bake once it's almost doubled.
- **Skip the bulk ferment:** you can shape right after mixing; the total fermentation time stays the same.
- **Same-day levain:** use 75g ripe starter + 75g water + 75g flour, and leave at 78–80°F for 3–4 hours. Or use ripe, active starter in place of the levain.
- **Temperature:** keep the dough at 78–82°F throughout. In a cool house (about 68°F), allow much longer rises.
- **Flour:** all-purpose can replace the bread flour.
- **Pan:** a bundt pan isn't required. An 8x8 pan or loaf pans work (possibly split between two pans).
- **Refrigerating after shaping:** the FAQ says assembled bread can sit in the fridge "a day or two." A staff reply in the comments warns the cinnamon can draw moisture out, so the dough may get wet after more than a few hours.
- **Freezing:** freezing shaped dough is not recommended (the creator says a week at most). Freeze baked leftovers airtight for up to 3 months.
- **Storage:** best within 24 hours; rewarm briefly in the microwave.
