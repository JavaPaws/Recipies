## Ingredients
- 250g freshly brewed espresso
- 100g warm water
- 100g active sourdough starter
- 30g cacao powder
- 480g bread flour, 12%+ protein (creator used King Arthur's)
- 10g salt
- ~¾ cup chocolate chips ("measure with your heart")

## Method
1. Combine the espresso, warm water and cacao and let it bloom. Add the starter, flour and salt. Mix until a shaggy dough forms.
2. Rest 30 minutes.
3. Stretch & folds: do fold #1, then fold in the chocolate chips during folds #2 and #3.
4. Bulk ferment 6–8 hours in a warm spot until doubled, or overnight in the fridge.
5. Final shape.
6. Preheat a Dutch oven at 450°F for at least 45 minutes.
7. Bake at 450°F: 30 minutes lid on, then 15 minutes lid off.
8. Rest 20 minutes before cutting.
