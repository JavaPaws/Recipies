# Coffee + Chocolate Sourdough

## Ingredients
- 100g active sourdough starter
- 8 espresso shots, cooled (about 170g)
- 2 tbsp brown sugar
- 190g filtered or bottled water
- 500g bread flour
- 10g salt
- 100g chocolate chips

## Method
1. Brew the espresso, stir in the brown sugar while hot until dissolved, then cool completely.
2. Mix the cooled espresso, water and starter together.
3. Add the flour and mix until no dry flour remains. Rest 30 minutes.
4. Add the salt and work it thoroughly into the dough.
5. Complete 4–6 sets of stretch and folds or coil folds, roughly 30 minutes apart, stopping once the dough feels smooth, elastic and holds itself together well.
6. Leave to bulk ferment until aerated, slightly domed and jiggly, with visible bubbles and a noticeable rise.
7. During lamination, gently stretch the dough out and scatter over most of the 100g chocolate chips. Fold the dough back over itself, then add the remaining chocolate chips during final shaping for even distribution.
8. Shape into a boule or batard and place seam-side-up in a banneton. Cold proof for 8–16 hours.
9. Preheat your oven and Dutch oven to 230°C for around 30 minutes.
10. Score and bake covered for 50 minutes at 230°C, then uncover and bake for another 10 minutes until deeply browned.
11. Cool for at least 1 hour before slicing.

## Notes
- ⚠️ From the creator: the brown sugar can make this dough ferment faster than usual sourdough, so watch the dough, not the clock.
- The creator replaced all the water with espresso from @mumsaidcoffee. The result is described as "rich, slightly sweet, chocolatey… with a proper espresso hit."
- 230°C ≈ 446°F (converted; the caption gives only °C).
