# Slow Cooker Loaded Baked Potato Soup

## Equipment
- 6–8 quart slow cooker
- Medium saucepan

## Ingredients
- 4 cups russet potatoes, peeled and diced into 1-inch pieces (about 4–5 large potatoes)
- 1 cup diced onion (about 1 small or ½ large)
- 3 cups chicken broth
- 4 tablespoons unsalted butter
- ¼ cup all-purpose flour
- ¼ cup sour cream (plus more for topping, if desired)
- 1½ cups heavy cream or half & half
- Salt to taste
- Ground black pepper to taste
- **Toppings:** cooked and crumbled bacon, shredded cheddar cheese, sliced green onions

## Method
1. Add the potatoes, onion and chicken broth to the slow cooker. Cover and cook on LOW for 4–6 hours or HIGH for 3–4 hours, until the potatoes are tender.
2. About 30 minutes before serving, melt the butter in a medium saucepan over medium heat. Whisk in the flour and cook until frothy.
3. Slowly whisk in the heavy cream and sour cream; it should thicken a lot as you whisk. Add the cream very slowly, whisking constantly, or it can stay thin.
4. Stir the cream mixture into the slow cooker. Cover and cook another 20–30 minutes, until thickened.
5. Season with salt and pepper. Top with bacon, cheddar and green onions.

## Notes
- **Texture:** for a smoother soup, mash some potatoes or use an immersion blender.
- **Lighter or lower-salt:** use half & half or whole milk instead of cream, and low-sodium broth and bacon.
- **Thicker or no flour:** use a slurry of 2 Tbsp cornstarch + 2 Tbsp cold water, added with the cream and sour cream. Without flour, skip the roux and use the slurry.
- **Frozen hash browns:** use about 1.5 lb; they need only about 3–4 hours.
- **More topping ideas:** chives, cherry tomatoes, jalapeños, avocado. A commenter stirred half the bacon into the soup.
- **Make ahead:** load the potatoes, onion and broth the night before and refrigerate covered. The potatoes may brown a little, which doesn't affect the flavor.
- **Leftovers:** fridge 3–4 days; freezer up to 3 months (thaw 24 h in the fridge). Reheat to 165°F, thinning with cream or broth.
