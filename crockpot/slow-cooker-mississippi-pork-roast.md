# Slow Cooker Mississippi Pork Roast

- **Servings:** 6
- **Times:** prep 5 min · cook 6 h on low · total 6 h 5 min

## Ingredients
- 2 pounds marinated fresh pork roast (a plain pork roast works too; see notes)
- 1 packet ranch seasoning
- 1 packet au jus gravy mix
- ½ stick butter (she used unsalted)
- 8–10 pepperoncini peppers

## Method
1. Put the pork roast in the slow cooker. Sprinkle the ranch and au jus packets on top, then top with the butter and peppers.
2. Cook on low for 6 hours.
3. Remove the pepperoncini, shred the meat with a fork, and serve.

## Notes
- **"Marinated" pork:** she used a pre-packaged marinated roast from the store, but says an unmarinated pork roast "turns out just as good." Any cut (shoulder, butt or loin) works; tenderloin works but is leaner.
- The peppers are mainly for flavor during cooking; you don't have to eat them.
- **Temperature:** the post cites 145–160°F internal, then a rest.
- Leftovers work well in sandwiches, tacos or nachos. A commenter added sliced onion, garlic and mushrooms.
- **Leftovers:** fridge 3–4 days; microwave to reheat.
- 84 kcal per serving (site figure, which looks low for a pork roast).
