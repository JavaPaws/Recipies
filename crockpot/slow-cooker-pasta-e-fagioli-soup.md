# Slow Cooker Pasta e Fagioli Soup

**Tags:** crockpot, soup, beef, pasta, beans

- **Source:** Instagram post (carousel, image 5 of 10) — https://www.instagram.com/p/DeABR_tFtze/
- **Creator:** @alyssa_therecipecritic (Alyssa Rivers | The Recipe Critic)
- **Full recipe:** https://therecipecritic.com/slow-cooker-pasta-e-fagioli-soup/

- **Sent by:** Brian, 2026-10-17
- **Servings:** 10 (1 cup each)
- **Times:** prep 10 min · cook 7–8 h on low or 3–4 h on high · total 7 h 10 min

## Ingredients
- 1 tablespoon extra virgin olive oil
- 1 pound lean ground beef
- 2 whole carrots, diced
- 4 celery stalks, diced
- 1 medium onion, diced
- 1 (28-ounce) can crushed tomatoes
- 2 (14.5-ounce) cans beef broth
- 1 tablespoon Italian seasoning
- 2 whole bay leaves
- Salt and pepper to taste (she used 1 tsp salt and ¼ tsp pepper)
- 1 (15-ounce) can cannellini beans, drained and rinsed
- 1 (15-ounce) can red kidney beans, drained and rinsed
- 1 cup ditalini pasta, uncooked

## Method
1. Heat the olive oil in a large skillet and brown the ground beef until no longer pink.
2. Put the beef in the slow cooker with everything else except the beans and pasta.
3. Cook on low for 7–8 hours or high for 3–4 hours. Thirty minutes before serving, stir in the beans and pasta.
4. Season with salt and pepper to taste. Discard the bay leaves.
5. Serve hot.
