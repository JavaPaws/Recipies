# Crockpot Parmesan Garlic Chicken Pasta

- **Times:** prep 5 min · cook 5–6 h on low or 3–4 h on high (card says 4 h) · total 4 h 5 min

## Ingredients
- 12 ounces Parmesan garlic sauce (see notes)
- 2 pounds boneless skinless chicken breasts
- 8 ounces cream cheese, cut into pieces
- ½ cup Parmesan cheese, grated
- 10 ounces dry pasta, cooked and drained (she used rotini; penne works)
- Parsley and red pepper flakes, for garnish
- Nonstick cooking spray

## Method
1. Spray the inside of the crockpot with nonstick cooking spray.
2. Place the chicken breasts in the bottom.
3. Pour the Parmesan garlic sauce over the chicken.
4. Add the cream cheese and Parmesan on top.
5. Cover and cook on low for 5–6 hours or high for 3–4 hours.
6. Shred the chicken and stir it into the sauce.
7. Cook the pasta according to the package directions.
8. Add the cooked pasta to the crockpot and stir. Garnish with parsley and red pepper flakes.

## Notes
- ⚠️ **Which sauce?** The recipe card says to pour in "the entire bottle," but in the comments she says the sauce is homemade ("You make it with this recipe!"). Her homemade version is probably https://therecipecritic.com/garlic-parmesan-sauce/, but I couldn't confirm that's the page the post links to, and I didn't fetch it. Commenters used bottled Buffalo Wild Wings garlic Parmesan sauce successfully.
- The chicken doesn't need seasoning; the sauce carries it.
- Use room-temperature cream cheese to keep it from curdling.
- Add a splash of chicken broth for a thinner sauce. Commenters rinsed out the sauce bottle with broth.
- **Variations:** chicken thighs instead of breasts; cayenne or more red pepper flakes for heat; lemon zest or juice to brighten; 1 cup sour cream or cottage cheese instead of the cream cheese.
- Use a 6–8 quart slow cooker.
- **Leftovers:** fridge up to 3 days; microwave about 1 minute, adding a little extra sauce if dry.
