# Crockpot Meatloaf

## Ingredients
### Meatloaf
- 2 pounds ground beef, no leaner than 85%
- ½ onion, chopped
- 3 tablespoons fresh parsley, chopped
- ¾ cup Italian bread crumbs
- ½ cup milk
- 1 tablespoon Worcestershire sauce
- 2 eggs
- 1 teaspoon garlic powder
- 1 teaspoon kosher salt
- ½ teaspoon pepper

### Glaze
- ½ cup ketchup
- 2 tablespoons balsamic vinegar
- 3 tablespoons brown sugar

### Also
- Foil and nonstick cooking spray (to line the crockpot)

## Method
1. Line the inside of the crockpot with foil and spray the foil with nonstick spray.
2. In a large bowl, combine the beef, onion, parsley, bread crumbs, milk, Worcestershire, eggs, garlic powder, salt and pepper. Mix with your hands until just combined; don't overmix.
3. Shape into a large oval and place it on the foil in the crockpot.
4. Whisk the ketchup, vinegar and brown sugar. Brush half on top of the meatloaf and save the rest.
5. Cook on low for 6 hours or high for 3 hours, until cooked through and 160°F inside.
6. Top with the remaining glaze when done.
7. Serve sliced, with any leftover glaze on the side.

## Notes
- Fattier beef (85% or less lean) keeps it moist. Ground turkey works, but add more liquid.
- Let it rest a few minutes before slicing.
- A commenter used 1 tsp onion powder instead of the onion.
- **Leftovers:** fridge 3–4 days; freezer 2–3 months (wrap in plastic, then foil). Reheat in the microwave for 1–2 minutes, or in the oven at 350°F for about 15 minutes.
