# Crockpot Tuscan Chicken

**Tags:** crockpot, chicken, freezer-friendly

- **Source:** Instagram post (carousel, image 4 of 10) — https://www.instagram.com/p/DeABR_tFtze/
- **Creator:** @alyssa_therecipecritic (Alyssa Rivers | The Recipe Critic)
- **Full recipe:** https://therecipecritic.com/crockpot-tuscan-chicken/
- **Posted:** 2026-10-02 (Instagram; part of her "9 Crockpot Recipes to Get You Through Crocktober" roundup); blog recipe updated 2023-06-19
- **Sent by:** Brian, 2026-10-17
- **Servings:** 6
- **Times:** prep 10 min · cook 3 h on high (or 5 h on low) · total 3 h 10 min

## Ingredients
- 4 boneless skinless chicken breasts
- 2 teaspoons Italian seasoning (homemade or store-bought)
- ½ teaspoon onion powder
- Kosher salt and cracked black pepper, to taste
- ½ cup chicken broth
- 1 cup heavy cream
- ½ cup sun-dried tomatoes, drained and chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tablespoon all-purpose flour
- ½ cup grated Parmesan cheese
- 1 cup chopped baby spinach
- Nonstick cooking spray (for the crockpot)
- Cooked pasta, for serving

## Method
1. Season both sides of the chicken breasts with the Italian seasoning, onion powder, and salt and pepper.
2. Spray the inside of the crockpot with nonstick cooking spray. Layer the seasoned chicken in the bottom.
3. In a mixing bowl, whisk together the chicken broth, heavy cream, garlic, sun-dried tomatoes, flour and Parmesan.
4. Pour the sauce over the chicken in the crockpot.
5. Cook on high for 3 hours, or low for 5 hours.
6. Right before serving, add the chopped spinach, replace the lid, and let it wilt for a few minutes.
7. Season with more salt and pepper if needed. Serve over cooked pasta.

## Notes
- **High vs. low:** the creator says low for 5 hours gives more tender, juicy chicken. Some commenters had the cream curdle on high. Her reply: it can break down if it sits too long at too high a heat.
- **Thicker sauce:** for each cup of sauce, mix 1 Tbsp cornstarch with 1 Tbsp cold water. Stir it in 10 minutes before serving and cook until thickened. Cornstarch can also replace the flour.
- **Swaps:**
  - Chicken thighs work in place of breasts.
  - Canned coconut milk works for dairy-free (it changes the flavor).
  - Frozen spinach: use ⅓–½ cup, thawed and squeezed, per 1 cup fresh.
- **Uncooked pasta in the pot:** add it in the last 30 minutes with 1 cup extra liquid per 1 cup of dry pasta (creator's reply in the comments).
- **Freezer meal:**
  - Season the chicken. Mix the cream, sun-dried tomatoes, broth, flour, Parmesan and garlic in a gallon freezer bag, add the chicken, press out the air, and freeze flat for up to 3 months.
  - Freeze the spinach in a separate bag.
  - To cook: thaw 1 hour on the counter, then cook on LOW for 5–6 hours, until the chicken reaches 165°F. Add the spinach 30 minutes before it's done.
- **Serving ideas from the post:** butter noodles, mashed potatoes, cauliflower rice, or zucchini noodles.
- **Leftovers:** fridge up to 4 days, airtight; freezer up to 3 months.
