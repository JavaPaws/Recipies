# Slow Cooker Creamy Ranch Pork Chops

## Ingredients
- 2 pounds boneless pork chops, thick cut (about 4–6)
- 6–8 medium red potatoes, cut into 2-inch pieces
- 1 (10.5-ounce) can condensed cream of chicken soup
- 1 (1-ounce) package dry ranch dressing mix
- Chopped parsley, for garnish (optional)
- Nonstick cooking spray

## Method
1. Spray the slow cooker insert with nonstick spray. Add the potatoes to the bottom.
2. Place the pork chops on top of the potatoes.
3. In a medium bowl, mix the soup and ranch mix. (No water is added to the soup.)
4. Pour the soup mixture over the pork chops. Cover and cook on LOW for 4–5 hours. Garnish with parsley if you like.

## Notes
- **Chops:** use thick-cut. Thin chops dry out, so start checking at 4 hours. Bone-in works, but timing may vary.
- **Potatoes:** red, Yukon gold or baby gold all work; cut them evenly.
- **Add-ins and swaps:**
  - Baby carrots, celery or onion can cook with the potatoes.
  - Cream of mushroom soup or homemade cream of chicken soup can replace the canned chicken soup.
  - Homemade ranch seasoning works in place of the packet.
  - A splash of milk thins the sauce.
- **Freezer meal:**
  - Mix the soup and ranch in a gallon freezer bag, add the chops and potatoes, coat them, press out the air, and freeze flat.
  - Thaw overnight in the fridge, then cook on LOW for 5–6 hours, until the pork reaches 145°F.
  - She says raw potatoes should freeze OK, or you can par-boil them first.
- **Dutch oven (her untested suggestion):** add 1 cup broth and bake covered at 325°F for 1.5–2 hours.
- Use a 6-quart slow cooker rather than a 4-quart.
- **Leftovers:** fridge up to 4 days; freezer up to 3 months. Reheat with a splash of broth or milk.
