# Crockpot Chicken Spaghetti

## Ingredients
- 3 chicken breasts (about 1½ pounds)
- 1 teaspoon onion powder
- ½ teaspoon garlic powder
- ½ teaspoon paprika
- ¼–½ teaspoon salt, to taste
- ¼ teaspoon ground black pepper
- 2 (10.5-ounce) cans condensed cream of chicken soup
- 1 (10-ounce) can diced tomatoes and green chiles, not drained
- 1 cup diced onion (about half a medium onion)
- 2 teaspoons minced garlic (about 2 cloves)
- 12 ounces spaghetti (weighed dry), cooked
- 1 cup shredded cheddar cheese
- ½ cup sour cream
- Cooking spray

## Method
1. Spray the crockpot with cooking spray. Add the chicken to a large (6–8 quart) slow cooker and season with the onion powder, garlic powder, paprika, salt and pepper.
2. Spread the soup, tomatoes and green chiles, onion and garlic evenly over the chicken. Cover and cook on LOW for 6–8 hours or HIGH for 4–5 hours.
3. Shred the chicken with two forks.
4. Add the cooked spaghetti, cheddar and sour cream. Stir until well combined.

## Notes
- **Pasta water:** reserve about 1 cup before draining. Add a few tablespoons when reheating leftovers to bring back the creamy texture.
- **Variations:**
  - Swap one can of cream of chicken soup for cream of mushroom.
  - Stir in broccoli, bell peppers, green beans or peas.
  - Use 4–6 oz cubed Velveeta instead of the cheddar.
- If it's too liquidy, a commenter drained the tomatoes. Cook the noodles to your preferred texture before adding them.
- **Make ahead:** assemble through step 2 and refrigerate the insert, covered, for up to 24 hours before cooking.
- **Leftovers:** fridge up to 5 days; reheat in 30-second microwave bursts. Freezing is not recommended.
