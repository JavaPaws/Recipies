# Slow Cooker Beef Bourguignon

**Tags:** crockpot, beef, stew

- **Source:** Instagram post (carousel, image 2 of 10) — https://www.instagram.com/p/DeABR_tFtze/
- **Creator:** @alyssa_therecipecritic (Alyssa Rivers | The Recipe Critic)

- **Servings:** 6
- **Times:** prep 20 min · cook 8–10 h on low or 6 h on high (card says 9 h) · total 9 h 20 min

## Ingredients
- 5 slices bacon, diced
- 3 pounds boneless beef chuck, cut into 1-inch cubes
- Salt and pepper (for the beef; amount not given)
- 1 cup red cooking wine
- 2 cups chicken broth
- ½ cup tomato sauce
- ¼ cup soy sauce
- ¼ cup flour
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 tablespoons minced fresh thyme (or 2 teaspoons dried)
- 5 medium carrots, sliced
- 1 pound baby potatoes (she used tri-color)
- 8 ounces fresh mushrooms, sliced
- Fresh chopped parsley, for garnish

## Method
1. In a large skillet, cook the bacon over medium-high heat for 5–7 minutes, until crisp. Add it to the slow cooker.
2. Salt and pepper the beef and brown it in the skillet, 2–3 minutes per side. Transfer it to the slow cooker.
3. Add the wine to the skillet, scraping up the browned bits. Let it simmer and reduce, then slowly add the chicken broth, tomato sauce and soy sauce. Slowly whisk in the flour. Pour the sauce into the slow cooker.
4. Add the garlic, thyme, carrots, potatoes and mushrooms to the slow cooker.
5. Stir well and cook on LOW for 8–10 hours or HIGH for 6 hours, until the beef is tender. Garnish with parsley; serve with mashed potatoes if you like.

## Notes
- **Wine:** you can leave it out, but she says the dish loses a lot of richness. A dry drinking wine can replace cooking wine; taste and adjust the salt, since cooking wine is salted. She suggests burgundy, Pinot Noir or Chianti.
- **Swaps and add-ins:**
  - Beef broth can replace the chicken broth.
  - She prefers cremini mushrooms.
  - Onions are optional: pearl onions, ½ chopped onion, or quartered or sliced shallots, added with the carrots and potatoes.
  - Bay leaves can be added.
  - ¼ tsp garlic powder can replace the fresh garlic.
  - Cornstarch can replace the flour for gluten-free.
- Don't overcook the bacon; it keeps cooking in the slow cooker.
- The liquid doesn't need to cover the vegetables; keep the lid on.
- If the stew ends up watery or greasy, simmer it uncovered to thicken and skim off the fat.
- **Instant Pot:** 35 minutes at high pressure (from her stovetop-version page).
- **Storage:** fridge 3–4 days; freezer up to 3 months. Reheat in the slow cooker on low for about 4 hours.
