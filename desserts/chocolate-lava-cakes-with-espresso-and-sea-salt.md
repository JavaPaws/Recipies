# Chocolate Lava Cakes with Espresso and Sea Salt

## Ingredients
- 1/2 cup (1 stick) unsalted butter, plus extra for greasing ramekins
- 6 oz high-quality dark chocolate (70% cocoa), chopped
- 2 large eggs + 2 egg yolks (at room temperature)
- 1/4 cup granulated sugar
- 1 tsp instant espresso powder (dissolved in 1 tsp hot water)
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 3 Tbsp all-purpose flour
- A pinch of salt
- Flaky sea salt and cocoa powder (for serving)

## Method
1. **Prepare ramekins:** Generously butter two ramekins and dust the insides lightly with cocoa powder, tapping out any excess.
2. **Melt chocolate & butter:** Melt the chopped dark chocolate and butter together in a heatproof bowl set over a simmering pot of water (double boiler), stirring until smooth and glossy. Remove from heat to cool slightly.
3. **Whisk base:** In a separate medium bowl, whisk together the whole eggs, egg yolks, and granulated sugar until pale and slightly thickened.
4. **Combine:** Gently whisk the dissolved espresso and vanilla extract into the melted chocolate mixture, then slowly fold the chocolate into the egg mixture until just combined.
5. **Fold flour:** Sift in the all-purpose flour and a pinch of salt, gently folding it in with a spatula until a smooth, rich batter forms.
6. **Bake:** Divide the batter evenly between the prepared ramekins and bake in a preheated oven at 220°C (425°F) for 10–12 minutes, until the edges are firm but the centers are still soft.
